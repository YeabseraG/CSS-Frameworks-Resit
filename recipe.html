Creamy Vegetable Pasta

An easy to make and comforting pasta recipe with fresh vegetables and a high degree of flexibility to adjust to your own taste, Perfect for a weekday dinner.

Ingredients

  • 250g pasta
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 150ml cream
  • Salt and pepper
